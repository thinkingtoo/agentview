# What you did with a session is one store: `marks`

2026-10-25. Candidate 7 of the architecture review of that day, settled in two rounds of
grilling with Thierry; the decisions below are his.

## Why

What agentview remembers per session lives in two modules and two files: `seen.py`
(`seen.json`, `{key: stop}`, no lock) drives the chime and `ready`; `finished.py`
(`finished.json`, `{"read": {key: stop}, "pinned": [key]}`, an `RLock`) drives `unread` and
session pins. One click (`fleet.mark_seen`) writes both files in two writes; the round
(`poll.round`) asks both for their keys and forgets in both; `fleet.live` loads both.
`seen.mark` and `seen.forget` share no lock, so a click and a round can lose one another's
write. Ticket 02 of the poll round left the two stores identical in shape (keys, forget the
keys given), which makes them cheap to join.

The facts stay apart. "Seen" (the chime's mark) and "Handover read" (`GLOSSARY.md`) are two
different stops per key: `fleet.look` marks a session seen without reading it when the stop
the page showed is older than the current one, and the old seen marks never finish a session.
Joining the stores joins the storage, not the facts.

On 2026-10-25 the live files held the same 6 keys with the same stops, no pins, and no bare
ids.

## Decisions

1. **One module, one lock.** `marks.py` owns every per-session mark: when it was seen, when its
   handover was read, whether it is pinned. One module-level lock covers every write.
   `seen.py` and `finished.py` are removed, with no shims; callers and tests move in the same
   change. No glossary entry: `marks` is storage, and "Handover read" and "Pin (a session)"
   keep their meaning.
2. **One file.** `marks.json`, beside the old two (`marks.store()` derives it from
   `XDG_STATE_HOME` as `seen.store()` did). One record per key:
   `{key: {"seen": stop, "read": stop, "pinned": true}}`, a field absent when the fact is.
   Session pins lose their order, which nothing read (`fleet.py` tests membership, the page
   gets a boolean).
3. **Import once, leave the old files alone.** The first `marks.load` that finds no
   `marks.json` builds it, under the lock, from `seen.json` and `finished.json`, prefixing a
   bare id with the default provider (the rule of `seen.migrate`), and writes it at once.
   `seen.json` and `finished.json` are never written or removed, so going back to the old code
   loses nothing; deleting them is a later chore. `seen.migrate` and its call in
   `fleet.py:178` go. A `marks.json` that exists but cannot be read loads as empty and is not
   rebuilt from the old files (as today: a broken file is a smaller problem than a page that
   will not load). Deleting `marks.json` by hand brings the old marks back; harmless, a
   changed stop is unread anyway.
4. **Interface.** Readers: `load(path=None) -> {key: record}`, `ready(key, stamp, state)`,
   `is_read(key, stamp, state)`, `pinned(key, state)`. Writers, all under the lock, each one
   read-modify-write of the file: `mark(key, stamp, read, path=None)`, the seen stop and, when
   `read`, the read stop, in one write (it replaces the two writes of `fleet.mark_seen`);
   `pin(key, on, path=None)`; `forget(drop, path=None)`, dropping exactly the keys given.
   And `keys(path=None)`. Writes never raise, as both stores do today.
5. **The click path stays outside `fleet.POLL`.** The store lock is enough against lost writes:
   `forget` re-reads under the lock and drops only the keys it was given, so a mark or a pin
   that lands mid-round survives. A click reaches only a live session (`fleet.find`), and the
   round never drops a live one. Taking `POLL` for a click would make it wait on a slow
   provider.
6. **One path parameter.** `fleet.live` and `poll.round` take `marks_path` in place of
   `seen_path` and `finished_path`. Tests patch `marks.store` or pass `marks_path`.
   `tests/test_paste.py:351` and `:768`, which iterate over the forget functions, iterate over
   one.
7. **One ticket.** The store alone is of no use and no halfway state is worth merging:
   `.scratch/marks/issues/01-one-marks-store.md`. Its last box restarts the service, with the
   KillMode drop-in checked first (memory `restarting-agentview-kills-sessions`), which also
   puts ticket 02 of the poll round live.
8. **Gate.** A test that fails on the tree before (the lost write of a click racing a forget;
   the import), Matt's `code-review` then `/codex-review` (Opus at once if Codex is out), the
   suite on t-compute over ssh (`git archive` of the head, private TMPDIR,
   `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`), and a live check after the restart: the page
   shows the same ready and unread tiles as before, and `marks.json` holds the 6 keys.

## Out of scope

Deleting `seen.json` and `finished.json`. The `provider:id` key parsed by hand in four places
(candidate 8, provider identity). The other review candidates.
